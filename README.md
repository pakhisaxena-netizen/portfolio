# Executive Portfolio – Pakhi Saxena

A bespoke, high-performance personal executive website and portfolio built for **Pakhi Saxena** (Partner – Retail & Consumer at Accord India / AltoPartners).

---

## 🌟 Highlights & Key Features

1. **Executive Hero & Visual Presentation**:
   - High-resolution, clean circular portrait extracted and framed in a luxury executive palette.
   - Distinctive positioning: Partner Retail & Consumer (Accord India / AltoPartners), Ex-Practice Head (Wazir Advisors, Redseer, KSA Technopak), and Corporate Leader (Aditya Birla, Arvind, Airtel, Star TV).
   - High-impact statistics bar (30+ Years Experience, 100+ C-Suite Mandates, 50+ Strategy & CDD Projects).

2. **Executive Narrative & Biography**:
   - Detailed story highlighting 3 decades of cross-functional excellence: from corporate operational leadership to management consulting practice head to executive search partner.
   - Core philosophy callout and executive profile summary.

3. **Strategic Advisory Pillars**:
   - Board & CXO Executive Search (CEO, CMO, COO, CDO)
   - Retail & CPG Strategy (Market entry, store rollouts, margin turnaround)
   - Private Equity Due Diligence (TAM validation, commercial DD)
   - D2C & E-Commerce Scaling (Tier-2/3 penetration, omnichannel integration)
   - Fashion, Apparel & Lifestyle (Denim, innerwear, ethnic wear, athleisure)
   - Organizational Transformation & Leadership Benchmarking

4. **Interactive Career Journey & Timeline**:
   - Accord India (AltoPartners Alliance)
   - Wazir Advisors
   - Redseer Strategy Consultants
   - KSA Technopak
   - Corporate Operations (Aditya Birla Fashion & Retail, Arvind Lifestyle, Airtel, Star TV, Bennett Coleman)

5. **Thought Leadership, Reports & Speaking**:
   - Interactive pop-up briefing modals for landmark reports (*The Future of Indian E-Commerce*, *Evolution of Apparel & Lifestyle*, and *Omnichannel Retail of the Future*).

6. **Credentials & Academics**:
   - Postgraduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) – Symbiosis Centre for Management & Human Resource Development (**SCMHRD**), Pune
   - Bachelor of Arts (Honours) in Economics – **University of Delhi**

7. **Interactive Features**:
   - **Dark Mode & Light Mode**: Seamless toggle with local storage persistence (defaults to sleek luxury executive dark mode).
   - **Print Executive Bio**: Dedicated `@media print` stylesheet that transforms the page into an executive one-pager CV/bio ready to save as PDF or print.
   - **Direct LinkedIn Integration**: Direct link to [LinkedIn Profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pakhi-saxena-4502316/).
   - **Advisory Inquiry Form**: Pre-formatted mailto builder and responsive form.

## 🚀 How to View Locally

Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in your default web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

Or run a local preview using Node.js:
```bash
npx serve .
```
