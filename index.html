Executive Narrative

Three Decades at the Intersection of Strategy, Operations & Leadership

Pakhi Saxena is a senior business strategy consultant and executive advisor with nearly 30 years of multidisciplinary expertise across India's consumer goods, modern retail, fashion apparel, and media landscape. Her career uniquely bridges the divide between hands-on corporate operations, top-tier management consulting, and C-suite talent leadership.

Currently serving as Partner – Retail & Consumer at Accord India (part of AltoPartners, the global executive search alliance across 35+ countries), Pakhi advises boards, visionary founders, and private equity sponsors on senior leadership architecture, CXO hiring, and executive succession planning across traditional retail, modern omnichannel brands, and hyper-growth D2C startups.

Prior to Accord India, Pakhi served as the Business Director & Practice Head for Retail & CPG at Wazir Advisors, as well as an Associate Partner with Redseer Strategy Consultants and KSA Technopak. During this extensive tenure in advisory, she spearheaded high-stakes commercial due diligence (CDD) for private equity funds, market entry frameworks for multinational brands, omni-channel scale-up roadmaps, and category repositioning strategies.

Her strategic perspectives are grounded in foundational operational stints with major conglomerates—including Aditya Birla Fashion & Retail, Arvind Lifestyle, Bharti Airtel, Bennett, Coleman & Co. (The Times Group), and Star TV. This rare blend of operational agility and boardroom clarity enables her to identify and nurture transformative leaders for today’s rapidly shifting consumer economy.
